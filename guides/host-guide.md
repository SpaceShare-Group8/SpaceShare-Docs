## Before You Begin

Before creating your listing, ensure you have:

- A verified SpaceShare Host account
- Photos of your workspace, including at least one clear cover photo
- Details about your power backup and internet setup, as these affect your reliability badge

---

## Creating Your First Listing

From your **Host Dashboard**, select **+ Add New Workspace**.

The listing form consists of three steps. Your progress is automatically saved as a draft (**Save Draft**, located in the top-right corner), allowing you to leave and return at any time.

### Step 1 — Basic Information

1. Enter your **Workspace Name** (for example, *The Workspace Hub*).
2. Select a **Workspace Type** from the dropdown menu.
3. Optionally, choose a **Business Category**.
4. Enter your **Location Address** by searching for the address or dropping a pin on the map.
5. Select your **City** and **State**.
6. Write a **Description** highlighting your workspace, available amenities, and nearby landmarks.
7. Select **Continue**.

> **Note:** Selecting **Cancel** at any stage discards the current draft.

---

### Step 2 — Amenities & Pricing

Select every amenity that is consistently available at your workspace.

| Category | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Power & Internet | High-Speed Wi-Fi, Fibre Internet, Generator, Solar Backup |
| Workspace & Facilities | Air Conditioning, Meeting Room, Private Office, Printer, Whiteboard |
| Comfort & Convenience | Parking, Security, Kitchen, Free Coffee, Restroom |

> **Important:** Your **Power & Internet** selections are used by the Trust Engine when determining your reliability badge. Only select amenities that are consistently available. False or inconsistent claims may trigger a review of your reliability badge.

After selecting your amenities, configure your pricing and availability, then select **Continue**.

---

### Step 3 — Photos & Verification

1. Upload a **Cover Photo** (required).
2. Optionally, upload up to **10 additional photos** to your **Workspace Gallery**.
3. Select your **Internet Provider** and **Power Source**.
4. Upload verification documents where applicable:
   - Business Registration (optional but recommended)
   - Utility Bill
   - Government-issued ID
   - Insurance Document
5. Select the checkbox confirming that you agree to the **SpaceShare Terms & Conditions** and **Host Agreement**.
6. Select **Submit Listing**.

Your listing will enter the review process before becoming visible in seeker search results.

## Setting Pricing & Availability

Pricing and availability are configured as part of **Step 2 (Amenities & Pricing).**

### Pricing

Complete the following fields:

- **Price per Hour (₦)**
- **Price per Day (₦)**
- **Capacity (People)** — Use the stepper control to specify the maximum number of occupants.
- **Minimum Booking Duration** — Select an option from the dropdown menu.

### Availability

Specify when your workspace is available for bookings.

- **Opening Time**
- **Closing Time**
- **Working Days** — Select the days your workspace is available, from Monday through Sunday.

> **Tip:** Once a booking is confirmed, the corresponding time slot immediately becomes unavailable to other seekers.

## Understanding Your Reliability Score

Your listing may display one of three trust badges.

| Badge | Color | How It Is Earned |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Host-Reported | Orange | Based on the amenities you declare during listing creation |
| Community-Verified | Green | Earned through positive feedback from seekers after completed bookings |
| SpaceShare-Verified | Blue | Awarded after a one-time verification of your workspace by SpaceShare |
