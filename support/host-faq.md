## Before You Begin

Before setting up your Corporate account, ensure you have:

- Your company's business details, including registration information if required
- A list of employee email addresses for team invitations

---

## Setting Up Your Account

1. Register a **Corporate** account. A Corporate account extends the standard **Seeker** experience with team management and budget controls. No separate application or login is required.
2. Complete your business verification details to identify your account as a Corporate account rather than an individual Seeker account.
3. Open your **Corporate Dashboard**, where you can manage employees, budgets, and usage reports separately from your personal bookings.

> **Note:** This section is pending verification against the Figma Corporate Setup screens. Field names and workflow details currently reflect the Product Requirements Document (PRD) and may be updated after design review.

---

## Inviting Employees

1. From your **Corporate Dashboard**, select **Invite Employees**.
2. Enter one or more employee email addresses.
3. Each employee receives an invitation to create a new SpaceShare account or link an existing one.
4. Once an employee joins your organization, eligible bookings can be billed to the corporate account instead of the employee's personal payment method.
5. Manage, resend invitations to, or remove employees at any time from the **Corporate Dashboard**.

> **Note:** This section is pending verification against the Figma Invite Employees screens. The final invitation workflow (such as bulk invitations, individual invitations, or role-based permissions) may change following design review.
