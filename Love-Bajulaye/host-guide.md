## Getting Started

### How do I create my first listing?
From your Host Dashboard, select **+ Add new workspace**. You'll complete a 3-step form: Basic Information, Amenities & Pricing, and Photos & Verify. Your progress saves automatically as a draft, so you can leave and return without losing work.

### How many photos do I need to upload?
A **Cover Photo** is required. Beyond that, you can add up to 10 more in your Workspace Gallery — optional, but listings with a fuller gallery tend to perform better in search.

### What happens after I submit my listing?
Your listing enters review before it appears in seeker search. If it's rejected, you'll receive a reason and can revise and resubmit.

### Can I edit or delete a listing after it's live?
Yes. Edit details, pause, or delete a listing at any time from your Host Dashboard.

## Amenities & the Trust Engine

### Why do my Power & Internet selections matter so much?
They're SpaceShare's core reliability signal. The checkboxes you select in Step 2 — Fast Wifi, Generator, Solar Backup, Fibre Internet, High-Speed WiFi — feed directly into the **Trust Engine**, which builds your listing's reliability badge.

### What is the Trust Engine, exactly?
A 3-tier badge system shown on your listing:

| Badge | Color | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Host-reported | Orange | Your declared amenities from Step 2 |
| Community-Verified | Green | Feedback from seekers after completed bookings |
| SpaceShare-Verified | Blue | One-time platform verification of your setup |

### Can I skip the amenity checkboxes and just describe my space in the Description field?
No. Power & Internet options are structured checkboxes in Step 2, separate from the free-text Description in Step 1. Structured data is what lets the Trust Engine compare listings and assign badges consistently.

## Pricing & Availability

### How does pricing work?
Set both a **Price per Hour** and a **Price per Day** (₦). Seekers choose whichever fits their session. You'll also set Capacity and a Minimum Booking Duration on the same step.

### How do I manage my availability?
Set your **Opening Time**, **Closing Time**, and **Working Days** in Step 2. This drives your live booking calendar — once a slot is taken, it's immediately unavailable to other seekers.

## Booking Requests

### How will I know when I've received a booking?
You'll get a push notification and in-app alert instantly. Your dashboard's Bookings and Calendar views show today's and upcoming bookings.

## Payments

### How much commission does SpaceShare take?
Each booking breaks down into a **Workspace Fee** (your rate), a **Service Fee**, and **VAT (7.5%)** — all shown to the seeker at checkout. Your payout reflects the Workspace Fee after the Service Fee is deducted.

### When and how do I get paid?
Your payout is credited to your in-app wallet (see **Payouts** in your dashboard sidebar). From there you can request a withdrawal to your bank account.

## Reliability & Flags

### What happens if my reliability score drops?
A low Community-Verified score can trigger a review flag on your listing, prompting an admin check.

### How do I recover a low reliability score?
Make sure your actual Power & Internet setup matches what you declared in Step 2. Completing SpaceShare-Verified status can also help rebuild trust while your Community-Verified score recovers.
